Astreveil Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in the current Android version of Astreveil, previously developed as HexaLume. The developer publishes through tvabi01-arms. For privacy questions, contact tvabi01@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The game saves progress and preferences on your device. These include completed levels, ship unlocks, earned upgrades, points, missile inventory, daily reward claims, and control, audio and graphics settings. These records allow the game to remember your progress and run its features.

The current version does not upload these saves to a developer-operated cloud service. Google Play Games sign-in, cross-device cloud restore and in-app purchases are not active in this version. A Settings entry for a planned feature does not mean that feature is connected.

Advertising and Google services

The Android game includes Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements, such as an extra life or a daily missile reward. Development builds currently request Google's test advertisements. Test advertisements do not mean that all SDK processing is disabled.

When Google advertising services are used, their SDK may collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as an advertising ID, app set ID and applicable identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, including ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, including crash information and performance data.

Google processes this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing can occur outside your country. Google describes its practices and retention in its Privacy Policy and information about partner sites and apps. See also Google's SDK data disclosures.

The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise GPS location for its own gameplay features.

Your advertising choices

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. You can continue playing without choosing an ad reward. Where Google presents a privacy message, review the available choices before selecting. In builds with the configured consent service, Settings shows Ad Privacy when Google requires a way to revisit those choices.

Consent messages for production advertising are still being configured in the current development version. The test-ad flow is separate from the planned production consent flow. You can also manage available advertising privacy controls in Android settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as a problem description or screenshot. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Keeping and deleting information

Local saves remain on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the game or otherwise remove the saved data. Clearing storage can permanently erase progress; this version has no cloud recovery. Deleting local saves does not delete information already processed by Google.

To ask about or request deletion of information you sent directly to us, email tvabi01@gmail.com. Requests concerning Google's records are subject to Google's privacy controls and policies. Information may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve an ongoing support issue.

Security

Local saves use the app's device storage. They are not proof of a paid purchase and should not be treated as a secure financial record. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Children and audience

Astreveil is intended for players aged 6 and above, including children, teenagers and adults. In the current internal testing build, advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment and a general-audience content rating for every player. Production advertising is not active.

Additional age screening and advertising safeguards are being prepared before public release. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns can contact us using the email below. This policy will be updated to describe the verified release settings before that version is published.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when information handling changes, including before introducing account linking, cloud saves or purchases. The date above identifies the latest revision.
