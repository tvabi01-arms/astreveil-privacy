HOVERVEIL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in the current Android version of HOVERVEIL. The developer publishes through tvabi01-arms. For privacy questions, contact tvabi01@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The game saves progress and preferences on your device. These include completed levels, ship unlocks, earned upgrades, points, missile inventory, daily reward claims, and control, audio and graphics settings. The game uses these records to remember your progress and run its features.

Optional Google Play Games and cloud backup

In the Google Play Android version, you can connect to Google Play Games and manually save or restore progress using the same Google account. Google provides the account authentication and Saved Games service. A backup contains completed levels, stars, points, ship unlocks, upgrades, inventory, reward-claim records, the selected ship and a backup timestamp. We do not receive your Google password.

Cloud backup is optional and requires a connection. Local play remains available without signing in. Save and restore ask for confirmation. Restoring replaces progress rather than merging points or inventory; a warning identifies missing local levels or stars. Before applying a restore, the game keeps a local recovery backup, accessible through Recover Local Backup in Account & Cloud Backup. This recovery copy is stored on the same device and is not a substitute for a separate cloud backup.

Google handles cloud-save storage under its service terms and Privacy Policy. Availability depends on the Play Games service and account. The game has no active in-app purchases.

Advertising and Google services

The Android game includes Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements, such as an extra life or a daily missile reward. Production builds can request live advertisements after the privacy checks described below. Builds explicitly configured for test inventory request Google's test advertisements; distribution through a private testing track alone does not enable test inventory. Test advertisements do not mean that all SDK processing is disabled.

When Google advertising services are used, their SDK may collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location.
  • Device and applicable account identifiers, such as app set IDs. The Android release removes the advertising ID permission; other SDK data processing can still occur.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, including ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, including crash information and performance data.

Google processes this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This processing can occur outside your country. Google describes its practices and retention in its Privacy Policy and information about partner sites and apps. The SDK's categories are documented in Google's advertising data disclosures.

The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise GPS location for its own gameplay features.

Your advertising choices

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. You can continue playing without choosing an ad reward. Where Google presents a privacy message, review the available choices before making a selection. In builds with the configured consent service, Settings shows an Ad Privacy option when Google requires a way to revisit those choices.

Before production ad requests, the game asks Google's User Messaging Platform to update privacy information and checks whether Google permits an ad request. Where required, Google's consent form is presented. If privacy eligibility cannot be established, live ads are unavailable. Ad Privacy in Settings lets eligible players revisit available privacy options or retry the privacy check. You can also manage available advertising privacy controls in Android settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as a description of a problem or an attached screenshot. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Keeping and deleting information

Local saves remain on your device until you clear the app's storage, uninstall it, or otherwise remove the saved data. Clearing storage can permanently erase local progress and the local recovery backup. A cloud restore is possible only if you previously saved a valid backup to the same Play Games account. Uninstalling or clearing local storage does not itself delete your Google cloud backup or information already processed by Google. Use the available Google Play Games and Google account controls to manage data held by Google.

To ask about or request deletion of information you sent directly to us, email tvabi01@gmail.com. Requests concerning Google's records are subject to Google's privacy controls and policies. Information may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve an ongoing support issue.

Security

Local saves use the app's device storage. They are not proof of a paid purchase and should not be treated as a secure financial record. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Children and audience

HOVERVEIL is intended for players aged 6 and above, including children, teenagers and adults. Before gameplay, the game asks for an age group: 6-12, 13-17 or 18+. It stores that choice on the device; it does not ask for a name or date of birth.

Unknown age groups cannot request advertisements. Child requests are tagged for child-directed treatment. Both child and teen requests are tagged as under the age of consent in the advertising and consent configurations. All age groups request a maximum ad content rating of G. Watching an advertisement is optional. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game's handling of information changes, including when adding services or changing advertising, account or cloud-save features. The date above identifies the latest revision.
